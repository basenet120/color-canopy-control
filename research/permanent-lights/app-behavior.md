# app-behavior (research pass 2026-09-26)

**HEADLINE:** For the Hampton Bay SQHDK03-15PSL (almost certainly built by Nanoleaf and powered by Hubspace), every look the official app offers runs on the controller itself once it is picked by one attribute write, and then needs no ongoing cloud traffic. That covers firmware presets, three per-puck "paint" custom patterns with motion/brightness effects and a speed setting, timed fades such as the 30-minute Sleep fade, and Party Mode driven by the controller's own microphone. What the app lacks is any way to sync or span several controllers: groups and rooms only send the same preset, colour or brightness to each member, and custom patterns and schedules are set per controller. It cannot treat the 9 kits as one 270-head canvas, so GOLDEN HOUR's wash moving across modules has to be driven step by step by our bridge, or the design has to accept that each module runs its own phase. The start offset and drift between controllers must be measured on the bench.

- [MEDIUM] **Consequence for GOLDEN HOUR: on-device motion follows the WIRING ORDER inside each module, not canvas X, and the A/B rotation reverses that order. A left-to-right amber wash across the whole canopy therefore needs bridge-orchestrated steps.**

INFERENCE: a firmware 'motion' effect will crawl along each module's serpentine and run mirror-reversed on B modules.
