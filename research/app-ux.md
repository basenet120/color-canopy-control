# app-ux

**HEADLINE:** The official Hubspace app (v2.5.1, Sep 2026) is a competent consumer remote but structurally wrong for a staffed retail activation — no user-composable scenes, per-device-only schedules, multi-user via password sharing, no iPad-native UI, no demo/retail/commercial mode, and 2025-26 reviews cluster on offline-on-launch lag, login failures, and group limitations — giving a bespoke kiosk/staff app a real, evidence-backed differentiation story.

## Findings
- [HIGH] **Current app versions and cadence: iOS 2.5.1 (released Aug 21, 2026) and Android 2.5.1 (Aug 22, 2026), with roughly monthly releases through 2026 (2.2.0 Feb 4, 2.3.0 Apr 15, 2.4.0 Jun 9, 2.5.0 Aug 18); Android changelogs are generic 'Bug fixes and Improvements'.**
  - Developer is Afero, Inc. (Google Play package io.afero.partner.hubspace). The steady cadence means any bespoke app riding the same cloud must be re-verified against monthly platform updates; an app-freeze on the show iPads/kiosk during Oct 20-Nov 1 is advisable.
  - src: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235 and https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/afero-inc/hubspace/
- [HIGH] **Hubspace 2.0 was a full app redesign shipped Nov 17, 2025 (iOS 2.0.0), announced by Afero Dec 16, 2025 and by Home Depot corporate Jan 7, 2026; it added Hubspace Connect (switches digitally control any Hubspace light regardless of wiring), circadian lighting, warm dimming, logarithmic dimming, and dynamic vacation mode (2.1.0, Dec 15, 2025).**
  - Marketing centers on wiring-free switch-to-light binding and wellness lighting, not on scenes, groups, or multi-user. Post-redesign complaints include added navigation depth ('Now i have to enter a new sub menu...making Hubspace cumbersome when it was perfectly fine') and notification clutter ('full of notifications for things I don't want', Dec 22, 2025 review).
  - src: https://corporate.homedepot.com/news/products/whole-home-integration-powered-hubspace and https://afero.io/html/home/article-Afero-launches-Hubspace.html
- [HIGH] **Structure: the app organizes devices by 'room and property' — multiple homes/properties per account with switching between them, and rooms within each; rooms and homes are cloud-side objects (the Afero API exposes metadevice.room and metadevice.home types).**
  - A featured App Store review specifically praises switching between multiple home locations, so a single staff account could technically model the 4 vignettes as 4 'homes' — but Home Depot's advice of separate accounts per vignette remains safer for blast-radius and session reasons. Rooms give unified on/off control that one 2022 reviewer called 'extremely handy'.
  - src: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235 and https://github.com/Emkraan/homeassistant-hubspace
- [HIGH] **Groups: users can create device groups (Edit Group flow to add/remove); a group of similar RGB lights exposes power, brightness, color temperature, RGB color and presets for the whole group; grouped devices remain individually controllable; NO group size limit is documented anywhere public.**
  - The biggest, most persistent group complaint through late 2025 is that groups cannot hold schedules: 'Absolutely need the ability to set a schedule for GROUPED items' (Oct 27, 2025, 3 stars), 'Schedules can only be applied to individual lights...I'll have 30 schedules by the time I'm done'. Nothing in the public record shows anyone running a 40-bulb group, let alone 200 bulbs on one account — behavior at Color Canopy scale is untested territory.
  - src: https://www.homedepot.ca/en/home/brand-pages/hubspace.html (grouping description via search) and https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235?see-all=reviews
- [MEDIUM] **Scenes: the app has NO user-composable multi-device scene feature (no 'capture these 40 bulbs' snapshot). What exists is per-device/group 'presets' — fixed theme patterns (e.g., Rainbow, Christmas; 18 themes on Hampton Bay string lights) plus saveable color presets that users report cannot be deleted; an update even removed custom-color selection for fade/rainbow effects.**
  - 2024-25 reviews: 'No ability to alter presets. No ability to create your own presets'; Nov 18, 2025 review complains of 'inability to delete saved color presets'; a search-surfaced review notes 'the lack of a way to save scenes'. One 2025-26 platform review (TheSmartHomie) claims 'scene creation for automations...single tap or voice command', which likely refers to Alexa/Google routines layered on top — verify in-app on 2.5.1. Guest 'choose a mood' UX therefore cannot be delivered natively by the official app: this is the single strongest pillar of the bespoke-app pitch.
  - src: https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1506042235/hubspace/reviews and https://smarthomiehub.com/platforms/hubspace/
- [HIGH] **Schedules/automations: per-device weekly time or sunrise/sunset schedules (location permission required); reviews complain there is no date option, effectively one schedule per device ('you can't have more than one schedule for any given device'), no group schedules, and no 'restore to current schedule' after power loss; vacation mode (randomized on/off) arrived in 2.1.0.**
  - Users report schedule execution failures too: 'Hubspace showed the schedule was active but it would not turn [on]' and sunrise/sunset misfires attributed to the cloud scheduler. For the activation, official-app scheduling is unusable at 200-bulb scale (200 separate schedules) — scene timing must live in the bridge/kiosk.
  - src: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235?see-all=reviews and https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1506042235/hubspace/reviews
- [HIGH] **Multi-user sharing is credential sharing, full stop: Home Depot's official guidance is to have family 'download the Hubspace app and then provide them your Hubspace credentials'; there are no invitations, roles, guest accounts, or per-device permissions. Multiple simultaneous sessions on one account are officially supported, and Manage Account > 'Where You're Signed In' lists active sessions.**
  - Reviews confirm the friction: 'No ability to invite a guest to your account...forces you to share login information'. MFA now exists on Hubspace accounts (the HA integration documents one-time codes, and notes enabling MFA does not invalidate existing tokens) — if a staffer enables MFA on a vignette account it would break shared iPad logins mid-show, so lock MFA off on show accounts. A Dec 2025 problem report describes 'account credential rejection when attempting to share across multiple devices'.
  - src: https://www.homedepot.com/c/ab/hubspace-faq/9ba683603be9fa5395fab9014e4790c0 (via search snippets; page 403s to bots) and https://github.com/jdeath/Hubspace-Homeassistant
- [HIGH] **Widgets/watch/web: iOS and Android home-screen widgets for device control arrived in v2.4 (June 9, 2026); there is no Apple Watch app (App Store listing supports iPhone, Mac with M1+, and Apple Vision Pro only) and no web app or browser control surface.**
  - Mobile-only control matters for the pitch: a bespoke kiosk/tablet web UI has no official counterpart. The widgets are per-device/group shortcuts, not scene walls.
  - src: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235
- [MEDIUM] **iPad is NOT a listed supported device: the App Store compatibility section lists iPhone (iOS 17.6+), Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14.6+), and Apple Vision Pro — no native iPad app, so on the four vignette iPads the official app runs as a scaled iPhone-compatibility app, and any iPad not updatable to iPadOS 17.6+ cannot run it at all.**
  - This is a concrete staff-UX weakness for the activation (letterboxed phone UI at arm's length) and a clean differentiation line for a bespoke iPad-first staff app. App size 119.9 MB iOS; languages English/French/Spanish.
  - src: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235
- [MEDIUM] **Offline behavior: Hubspace markets BLE fallback ('the only smart home solution that allows you to continue controlling your products locally with Bluetooth, in case your Wi-Fi ever goes out'), but BLE control requires the phone in radio range, responds in 'a couple of seconds' per device vs near-instant on Wi-Fi, and community integrations flatly state cloud control 'needs...the Hubspace servers'.**
  - BLE fallback is per-phone, per-device, point-to-point — it cannot restore a 40-bulb zone scene quickly and is irrelevant to the kiosk bridge (cloud-only via aioafero). For the <60s recovery acceptance target, the official app's BLE path is at best a manual staff workaround for a single hero bulb, not a mitigation.
  - src: https://smarthomeace.com/how-to-use-smart-bulb-without-wifi/ (via search snippets) and https://github.com/jdeath/Hubspace-Homeassistant
- [HIGH] **Top recurring pain point #1 — stale/false 'offline' states: on opening the app 'the bulb/device is listed as offline for a long time' (Nov 18, 2025, 1-star), multiple 2025 reports of 'products showing offline despite having power' and lights 'marked offline while appearing functional; manual controls unresponsive'.**
  - JustUseApp's live problem mix (70 reports in 24h, fetched Sep 19, 2026): connection issues 27.0%, 'not working' 19.8%, login problems 11.7%, other 36.9%. The mechanism is visible in community code: per-device state polling with stale poll responses overwriting optimistic updates — the same cloud the kiosk bridge will use, so the kiosk UI must render optimistic state, not wait for cloud truth.
  - src: https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1506042235/hubspace/problems
- [HIGH] **Top recurring pain point #2 — login/session failures: 'Hubspace logged us out and somehow one of the fan lights turned itself on'; Jan 2026 report that the app 'keeps going out' and won't permit sign-in; login problems are 11.7% of live problem reports. Under the hood, auth is Keycloak OAuth2+PKCE at accounts.hubspaceconnect.com with access tokens lasting only ~118 seconds, refreshed continuously.**
  - Any refresh hiccup or Afero-side auth outage surfaces as a logout or all-devices-offline event. HowToGeek noted the platform had NO 2FA as of March 2024; MFA arrived later (now documented in community integrations). For the show: pre-login all devices, keep sessions warm, and bench an 8h soak for silent logouts.
  - src: https://github.com/Emkraan/homeassistant-hubspace and https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1506042235/hubspace/problems
- [MEDIUM] **Top recurring pain point #3 — command errors and reversion: users hit 'we ran into an error, please try again later' when changing colors right after opening the app, and the community integrations document a historically common quirk where 'a command seems to revert a few seconds after you set it' (stale poll overwriting the new state).**
  - For a 40-bulb zone scene this manifests as a few bulbs snapping back to the old color seconds after Apply — directly threatens the 'zero wrong bulbs across 100 scene changes' acceptance target regardless of whether the official app or the bridge sends the command, since both ride the same cloud state machine. The bridge should re-assert state after ~5s as a belt-and-suspenders pass.
  - src: https://github.com/Emkraan/homeassistant-hubspace and https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1506042235/hubspace/reviews
- [MEDIUM] **How the app behaves changing 50+ bulbs at once is UNDOCUMENTED: no public review, forum post, or article shows anyone driving 50+ Hubspace bulbs from the app; no group-size or per-account device caps are published; and whether a group action is one cloud call or N per-device calls is not publicly known.**
  - Adjacent evidence points to staggered ('popcorn') behavior: the generic smart-home popcorn effect comes from per-device unicast fan-out, Afero 'rate-limits aggressively' (the HA integration floors state polling at 2s for this reason), and the aioafero library has no group/scene/room controllers at all — every community command path is per-device. The aioafero project ships MITM tooling (docs/mitm/) that can capture what the official app actually sends for a group action; running it answers the one-call-vs-N-calls question before the Sep 23 gate.
  - src: https://aioafero.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ and https://api.github.com/repos/Expl0dingBanana/aioafero/git/trees/main?recursive=1
- [HIGH] **There is no demo mode, retail mode, guest mode, or kiosk mode in the Hubspace app — nothing in the public record (listings, FAQs, reviews, press) suggests any such feature exists. 'Party Mode' is a music-sync lighting effect on specific string-light SKUs (e.g., Hampton Bay 12-Light HB-1024SC-BK), not a demo feature.**
  - Guest-facing interaction with the official app means handing over an authenticated session with full account power (delete devices, rename rooms, change Wi-Fi). Any guest-safe experience requires either heavy iPad guided-access lockdown of the official app or a bespoke front end — a core pitch point.
  - src: https://www.homedepot.com/p/Hampton-Bay-Smart-Black-12-Light-24-ft-Indoor-Outdoor-Plug-in-Integrated-LED-RGBW-String-Light-with-Party-Mode-Powered-by-Hubspace-HB-1024SC-BK/327262856
- [HIGH] **Home Depot offers NO commercial/pro Hubspace tooling: no fleet console, no property-management portal, no public API, no multi-unit dashboard. The Jan 2026 corporate announcement mentions Pros exactly once ('tailored for both everyday customers and Pros alike') with zero pro-specific features; Afero's enterprise IoT PaaS is sold to manufacturers/brands (like Home Depot), not to site operators or businesses.**
  - The only quantified operational claim in Afero's Home Depot case study is device onboarding in under 60 seconds via QR scan, with Dekra/ioXT security certification. This vacuum is the commercial opening for the bespoke app pitch: 'the world's largest home improvement retailer has no operator-grade control surface for its own smart home platform.'
  - src: https://corporate.homedepot.com/news/products/whole-home-integration-powered-hubspace and https://www.afero.io/html/home/case-study-home-depot.html
- [MEDIUM] **Ratings are high and stable, not degrading: iOS 4.8/5 from ~223K US ratings (≈252.5K aggregate per MWM tracker, 2.5M+ downloads), Google Play ≈4.58/5 from ~41K ratings with ~35K downloads/month; the app holds top-20 House & Home category positioning in Canada; no evidence of a post-2.0 ratings collapse.**
  - The pitch should NOT claim 'the app is hated' — headline sentiment is positive, anchored on effortless QR setup and reliability for small installs. The honest framing: excellent consumer app for 5-20 devices; the complaint clusters (group schedules, scenes, sharing, offline lag) become disqualifying only at commercial/activation scale.
  - src: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hubspace/id1506042235 and https://www.appbrain.com/app/hubspace/io.afero.partner.hubspace and https://mwm.ai/apps/hubspace/1506042235
- [HIGH] **Support channel is consumer-grade and closed on one tour day: Hubspace phone support (1-877-592-5233) runs M-F 8am-7pm, Sat 9am-6pm EST — closed Sundays (tours run Oct 23-25, including Sun Oct 25), and reviews complain 'no app support...very few resources online' and 'no real way to reach out to anyone'.**
  - Plan for zero vendor support during the Sunday tour window and the Oct 29 evening event (support closes 7pm EST weekdays). Escalation paths must be internal (bridge restart runbook, spare paired bulbs).
  - src: https://www.homedepot.com/c/ab/hubspace-faq/9ba683603be9fa5395fab9014e4790c0 (hours via search snippets) and https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1506042235/hubspace/reviews
- [HIGH] **String-light specifics for the glow garden vignette: Hampton Bay cafe string lights (Novolink OEM) support a max of 5 daisy-chained strings per controller, ship 18 fixed preset themes, require 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, and keep running their last preset autonomously if the app/cloud disconnects.**
  - The 'lights operate independently if not connected to the app with preset colors active' behavior is actually a resilience asset for the vignette: pre-stage the look, and a cloud blip won't kill it. Multiple controllers are grouped in-app for synchronized effects.
  - src: https://www.novolinkinc.com/hampton-bay-rgbw-cafe-string-light-faq
- [HIGH] **The bespoke app cannot replace the official app for pairing: device onboarding (QR scan, Wi-Fi provisioning) is proprietary to the official app and is its best-loved feature ('no setup wizards, no pressing and holding any buttons, no hassles'); a bespoke app rides the same account/cloud after pairing.**
  - Pitch framing: bespoke = operation layer (scenes, kiosk, staff iPad control, monitoring), official app = provisioning tool used once during install week. This also bounds the bespoke product's scope honestly for the client: it is a control surface, not a full platform replacement, and it depends on an unofficial API (Afero could change auth/endpoints at any monthly release).
  - src: https://www.howtogeek.com/home-depots-hubspace-still-needs-work-to-compete-in-smart-home/

## Bench tests
- Popcorn baseline: put 40 bulbs in one Hubspace app group on one account; tap a color change in the official app while filming the grid at 60/240fps; measure tap-to-first-bulb latency and first-to-last-bulb spread over 20 trials; repeat with the aioafero bridge issuing parallel per-device commands to the same 40 bulbs, and compare against the <2s spread target.
- Group-action wire capture: use aioafero's documented MITM tooling (docs/mitm/) to capture what app 2.5.1 sends for a 40-bulb group color change — one group-object call or 40 per-device calls — and whether the cloud applies them staggered; this determines if official-app groups are even a viable fallback control path.
- 200-device account stress: enroll 200 bulbs on a single Hubspace account; measure app cold-start to usable home screen, time until tiles stop showing false 'offline', scroll performance, and any silent cap/throttle; then repeat with the recommended 5-account split (40 bulbs each) to validate Home Depot's separate-accounts advice.
- Command-reversion watch: after each applied scene, record each 40-bulb zone for 15 seconds and count bulbs that revert to the prior state (documented 'command reverts a few seconds after you set it' quirk); verify whether a bridge-side re-assert pass at t+5s eliminates visible reverts.
- 8-hour concurrent-session soak: sign one vignette account into two iPads plus the kiosk bridge simultaneously (MFA off), alternate control actions every few minutes for 8h, and log forced logouts, 401s/token-refresh failures, or 'credential rejected' events; confirm expected sessions in Manage Account > Where You're Signed In.
- iPad reality check: install the official app on the actual staff iPads; confirm iPadOS >= 17.6, whether it runs letterboxed in iPhone-compatibility mode, and time a staff task (change holiday living room group to a target color) — tap count and seconds — as the before/after demo for the bespoke app pitch.
- Offline/recovery drill: cut venue WAN; verify the official app's Bluetooth fallback from kiosk position (per-device latency, effective range, whether a 40-bulb zone can be restored at all); restore WAN and measure time until cloud control works again versus the <60s recovery target.
- MFA and preset hygiene: on a fresh show account confirm MFA can remain disabled and no forced-2FA prompts appear; then save/modify color presets on 2.5.1 and verify whether the can't-delete-preset defect still exists and whether preset clutter accumulates across grouped bulbs.

## Open questions
- Does an official-app group action produce ONE cloud call (group metadevice write with cloud fan-out) or N per-device calls? Nothing public answers this; MITM capture is the only way, and it sets the popcorn floor for any control path.
- Is there an undocumented per-account device cap or throttling threshold near 200 devices on the Afero/Hubspace consumer tier? No limits are published; Afero support or bench loading is the only source.
- Did any 2.2-2.5 release (Feb-Aug 2026) quietly add a scene/automation builder under another name? Android changelogs are generic 'Bug fixes and Improvements'; only an in-app walkthrough of 2.5.1 settles the scenes question definitively (one 2025-26 review claims 'scene creation' exists, contradicting user reviews).
- Can MFA be guaranteed OFF for the life of the show accounts, and does Afero ever force-enroll MFA or expire refresh tokens on dormant/new accounts (which would break shared iPad logins and the kiosk bridge mid-show)?
- What is the month-by-month ratings trajectory since the Nov 2025 2.0 redesign (needs Appfigures/Sensor Tower data or manual review pagination — headline averages look stable but redesign-era 1-star clusters are visible in Dec 2025 reviews)?
- Does the app's false-'offline'-on-launch lag scale with device count (20 devices vs 200), and does it also afflict the API state the kiosk bridge reads at boot?
